## What changed for the owner

- **Gone, with a reason:** Used up, Thrown away or Given away. A gone item leaves the catalog, the Expiry page, reminders and the Inbox; nothing is deleted.
- **Put back** undoes it and everything returns.
- **Gone items** in the catalog shows what is gone and why.
- **One-tap quantities:** −1 and +1 on counted items; an amount such as 500 ml opens a quick edit (*Amount left*). *No count yet* / *No amount yet* when empty.
- A perishable item whose date you clear shows again as *No date* on the Expiry page; only Non-perishable items are counted quietly. SG-271's small hint text is now 13 px.
- The Inbox count and pages no longer include a gone item's tasks.

**None of this has been used live; the first real use is the owner's.**
